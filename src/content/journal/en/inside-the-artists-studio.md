A museum shows you what a culture has decided to keep. A studio shows you what it is still deciding. That is why, in every city, I look for the doors that open onto work in progress.

## The museum as a starting point

Buenos Aires has an extraordinary circuit of institutions — MALBA, the Museo Nacional de Bellas Artes, the Colección de Arte Amalia Lacroze de Fortabat, and Fundación PROA facing the old port of La Boca. They give you the vocabulary. The studios give you the conversation.

## What a studio visit offers

- **Process.** Sketches, failed attempts, materials — the thinking behind the finished piece.
- **Context.** Artists are the best interpreters of their own neighbourhoods.
- **Encounter.** An hour of genuine exchange stays with you longer than a day of sightseeing.

> The most memorable rooms in a city are rarely the ones with ticket desks.

## Etiquette, briefly

Arrive on time, ask before photographing, and come with curiosity rather than a shopping list. If something moves you, ask about it — acquisition is welcome, never expected.
