The Sierras de Córdoba are where Argentines go to slow down. Low mountains, clear rivers and villages that keep their own pace make it an ideal first route for travelers who prefer depth to distance.

## Day 1 — Córdoba city

Arrive and walk the historic centre, including the Manzana Jesuítica, part of a UNESCO World Heritage site. Dinner in the Güemes neighbourhood.

## Day 2 — Alta Gracia

A short drive to the Jesuit estancia of Alta Gracia and its reflecting pool. Afternoon at leisure; the town rewards an aimless walk.

## Day 3 — Towards Traslasierra

Cross the Altas Cumbres road, one of the most beautiful drives in the region. Settle into a small lodge for two nights.

## Day 4 — Nono and the valley

Slow morning, a visit to the Museo Rocsen, and a long riverside lunch. Sunset from the hills.

## Day 5 — Return

A last breakfast with a view, and back to Córdoba for your onward flight.

> The route is short on kilometres and long on hours — exactly as it should be.
